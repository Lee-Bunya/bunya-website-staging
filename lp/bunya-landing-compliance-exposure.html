Quick answers

Isn't consent capture enough?

No. Consent is the client agreeing to pay; it says nothing about whether the service was delivered. Under audit, delivery is the question that triggers remediation — and it's the half most firms can't quickly prove.

Does this replace what our CRM already does?

It complements it. Your CRM captures consent well — Bunya adds the continuous proof of service delivery, and assembles the audit evidence, which is the gap the major platforms leave open.

Is this legal advice or indemnity?

No. Bunya makes service-vs-fee delivery visible and provable and keeps the evidence trail; it doesn't remove an adviser's underlying obligations or provide legal advice. AI drafts, a human approves, the system records both.
