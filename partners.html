Why we partner

The part of the story Bunya doesn't build.

Bunya makes your firm run smoothly and grow. But there's a further question every principal eventually faces: is the business an asset that could run — or sell — without you?

That's a discipline of its own, and it's not something we build. So we partner with the people who do it best. Adapt helps owner-led businesses move from owner-run to owner-independent — lifting leaders, documenting how the firm runs, and designing out the dependency that holds a firm's value down. It's the natural complement to a practice Bunya already runs and grows.

And it's a natural fit: several advice firms already work with both. Adapt's own clients include wealth and advice practices across Australia — the same kind of firms Bunya is built for.
