What it is

The conversation ends. The work begins.

Every client call and meeting holds decisions, actions and obligations — and in most firms that value leaks the moment the call ends, scattered across memory, scribbled notes and someone's inbox.

Bunya Voice closes that gap. Business calling and Teams meetings run through one Microsoft workflow, every conversation captured to SharePoint and linked to the client record, with notes and a branded follow-up drafted for a human to review. Nothing lost between the conversation and the action.
