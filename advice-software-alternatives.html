The alternatives, compared fairly

Advice software alternatives, compared

Xplan, Plutosoft, Worksorted, AdviserLogic, Midwinter and the new AI tools — all good at what they do, all rented. Here's how they compare, and where Bunya offers a different model.

The short answer

The leading advice-software platforms in Australia are Xplan (the incumbent), Plutosoft, Worksorted, AdviserLogic and Midwinter — plus newer AI tools like Intelliflo and SoA-automation add-ons. They're all good at what they do, and they're all platforms you rent per seat. Bunya is the different option: a command centre built to your firm, in your own Microsoft 365, that you own outright — replacing the CRM-plus-a-stack-of-tools with one system.

If you're comparing advice software, it helps to sort the field by what kind of thing each one is — because most "alternatives" are variations on the same model, and one is genuinely different.

The established platforms

Xplan (Iress) — the incumbent, ~60% market share, the most comprehensive feature set in Australia. Deep, but widely described as slow, dispersed and rising in price. See our Xplan alternative page for detail.

Plutosoft — the highest-rated dedicated CRM (top NPS three years running), streamlined, with fast SoA production. An excellent CRM if a clean, well-liked CRM is what you need.

Worksorted — genuinely liked, strong workflow and agreement tracking, a great fit for smaller independent practices.

AdviserLogic & Midwinter — modern, cloud-native platforms; AdviserLogic strong on compliance integration, Midwinter (AdviceOS) strong on scaled-advice modelling.

All four are solid, modern products. What they share: you rent them per seat, your data lives on their platform, and they're configured to you rather than built around your firm.

The AI wave

Intelliflo and DASH are building AI-native platforms (meeting capture, fact-find completion, SoA drafting). A newer group of tools — Zatersio, Asendium, Ignition Wealth and similar — focus specifically on automating the Statement of Advice, often sitting on top of Xplan or AdviserLogic. They can genuinely cut SoA time. But they add another subscription to the stack, and the platform underneath is still rented.

Almost every alternative asks: which platform should we rent instead? Bunya asks a different one: should we keep renting at all?

How Bunya fits — the different model

Bunya isn't a better rental. It's a command centre built to your firm's exact way of working, running inside your own Microsoft 365, that you own outright — with the CRM, the review engine, one-click advice production, revenue, reporting and compliance in one connected system rather than a platform plus a stack of add-ons.

Key takeaways

  • Most alternatives (Plutosoft, Worksorted, AdviserLogic, Midwinter) are good platforms you rent per seat.
  • SoA-automation tools (Zatersio, Asendium, Ignition) speed up documents but sit on top of a rented platform.
  • Bunya is a different model: one owned system, built to your firm, in your own Microsoft.
  • The real choice is rent-a-different-platform vs stop-renting-and-own-one.
