What it is

Marketing that runs, without the compliance fear.

Most advice firms don't market because it's slow, and because saying the wrong thing feels risky. Broadcast HQ removes both problems: it starts from your business goal, generates a whole campaign, and runs every piece through your compliance guardrails before a human approves it.

It isn't a bolt-on social tool. It's the marketing engine inside your command centre — drawing on the same brand, the same data, the same system you own. One idea in; a compliant, multi-channel campaign out.

A closer look

Your judgement, made reusable.

Capture → Shape

From one thought to a full set of assets

Drop in an idea, a market event, or a business goal. Broadcast HQ shapes it into a coordinated campaign across the channels that matter — each drafted to your brand voice, ready to review.

  • No blank page, no starting from scratch
  • Consistent brand and message across channels
  • Drafted for approval, never auto-sent
◉Swap: idea-to-campaign view
Govern

Compliance judges in the workflow

Your compliance rules and brand guardrails are built into the flow — so risk is checked as the campaign is made, not discovered after it's posted. The firm sets the rules once; every campaign respects them.

  • Guardrails applied to every piece
  • Firm-level control over what can go out
  • An evidence trail of what was approved
◆Swap: compliance guardrails view
The two layers

Advisers post; the firm governs

Advisers get self-serve reach on their own channels — personal, authentic, effective — while the firm governs the rules, voice and compliance behind them. Personal reach without losing control.

  • Adviser layer: self-serve, own channels
  • Firm layer: rules, voice, compliance
  • One system, two levels of control
