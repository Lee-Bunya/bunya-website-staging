The onboarding playbook

From a raw client folder to a paraplanner handover — in five supervised stages.

The biggest skill in the system takes a new client through the whole advice-preparation pipeline. It does the careful reading and drafting between each stage — and stops at a gate after every one, where an adviser approves before it continues.

Stage 1

Intake & file-note review

Classifies documents, parses the fact find, runs the checks, and flags real inconsistencies in the notes.

Gate 1 · you approve
Stage 2

Strategy paper & fees

Drafts a brand-compliant, multi-section strategy paper and populates the fee calculator — flagging fees over threshold.

Gate 2 · you approve
Stage 3

BID & Code of Ethics audit

Audits the whole file against the Best Interests Duty and all twelve Code of Ethics standards, pinning each gap to a rule.

Gate 3 · you approve
Stage 4

Pre-paraplanner file audit

A final pass over the assembled file, turning everything still open into one ranked issues register.

Gate 4 · you approve
Stage 5

SoA handover pack

Assembles the strategy request, authority to proceed and a file manifest — and stops before anything is sent or filed.

Gate 5 · you authorise

The part that matters most

The AI does the work between the gates. You make every decision that matters.

This isn't "AI drafts, a human approves" as a slogan — it's control built into every step. Here's what that means in practice.

Nothing changes without your say-so

It proposes specific edits with reasons, then waits. You accept, remediate or override — the next stage stays blocked until you do.

Your originals are never touched

Approved edits are written to dated copies. It won't rename or move a file in SharePoint without stopping to ask you first.

Honest about what it doesn't know

Where the data is missing it writes "needs confirmation" — it never invents a name, a date or a figure to fill a gap.

Hard-stops it won't let you skip

Some compliance steps can't be overridden. On a real file, the pipeline won't hand over until they're genuinely resolved.

AI skills

Your data. Your logic. Your AI.

A core part of the system: custom AI skills that run against the firm's own data to draft advice work — with a human always in the loop. Skills are built to the firm's own logic, and can be created by the firm itself via Cowork.

  • Insurance needs analysis from file notes, Teams and Outlook data
  • A compliance / audit skill against Best Interest Duty and the Corporations Act
  • Strategy papers and a built-in fee calculator from your own logic
  • Document generation — PDFs, spreadsheets, PowerPoints — on your data

Compliance & data control

Audit-ready. Firm-owned.

Core documents — the SoA, the Authority to Proceed — are stored with review threads and revenue tracked through the system. Staying inside the Microsoft ecosystem is exactly what keeps the firm in control of its own data lake, and what unlocks the AI.

  • Evidence chains keyed to compliance-manual clauses
  • Your data lake, not a third party's centralised one
