All your questions, answered ↗
Does Bunya replace our CRM?

The Command Centre has a CRM built in. For most firms, it replaces the standalone CRM and the tools around it. Where needed, it can work alongside a system you keep, including one your licensee requires.

Where does our data live?

In your own Microsoft 365 environment, under your firm's governance. The system connects with the Microsoft tools your team already uses, including SharePoint, Outlook and Teams.

Does AI make decisions for our advisers?

No. AI helps prepare the work; an adviser reviews and approves. Advice drafts and proposed changes go through human approval before they reach a client or are saved.

What does “own the system” mean?

The system is built in your tenancy and belongs to your firm. Your Microsoft licensing and any agreed ongoing support still apply. We'll walk through the build, licensing and support costs with you.

Can we see it before committing to a build?

Yes. Start with a demo, then explore a Blueprint using your practice's data before committing to the full build. The Blueprint is yours to keep and its cost is credited if you proceed.
