Bunya vs Fin365

Fin365 is the closest thing to Bunya — also Microsoft-native, also adviser-built, also in your own tenancy. Here's the honest, precise difference.

The short answer

Fin365 and Bunya are the two most similar options in Australian advice software — both built on Microsoft (Dynamics / Power Platform), both installing into your own environment, both adviser-built. The difference is depth and model: Fin365 is a configured CRM product you subscribe to; Bunya is a whole command centre built to your firm's exact way of working, with a core AI skills engine, that you own outright.

Most advice-software comparisons are easy because the products are so different. Fin365 is the exception — it's the closest thing to Bunya in the market, so the comparison deserves to be precise and fair.

What Fin365 gets right

Fin365 is a genuinely strong, Australian, adviser-built platform — and credit where it's due:

  • It's Microsoft-native: built on Dynamics 365, Power BI and Azure, and it installs into the firm's own Office 365 environment.
  • It integrates with Outlook, Teams and SharePoint, with Power BI reporting and a client portal.
  • It has real compliance and workflow depth, built by people who know advice.

If you're choosing between Fin365 and a traditional rented CRM like Xplan, Fin365's Microsoft-native, in-your-tenancy approach is a genuine point in its favour — for the same reasons it's a point in Bunya's.

Because both are on Microsoft, "we're in your tenancy" isn't the difference. The difference is what's built on top, and whether you own it.

Where Bunya goes further

The similarities are real, so the distinctions matter:

  • Built to your firm, not configured from a product. Fin365 is a Dynamics-based product you configure. Bunya is built around your firm's exact processes — the system shaped to you, not you to the system.
  • An AI skills engine at the core. Bunya leads with AI as a core capability — one-click fact find, SoA and RoA, plus firm-buildable skills. Fin365 has been publicly more cautious on AI ("hasten slowly"). We lead where the market is heading rather than waiting.
  • A whole command centre, not a CRM. Bunya runs the whole practice — reviews, jobs, advice production, revenue, compliance, plus modules like Bunya Voice and Broadcast HQ. Fin365 positions primarily as the CRM.
  • You own it outright. Bunya is built and licensed to you as an owned asset, not an ongoing product subscription.
